01 / RESEARCH

Questions from clinical practice

My research interests centre on how imaging, biopsy and prediction models can inform decisions in prostate cancer diagnosis.

01

MRI & targeted biopsy

Studying the role of prostate MRI and targeted biopsy in the diagnostic pathway, with attention to the clinical questions they are intended to address.

  • Prostate MRI
  • Targeted biopsy
02

Clinical prediction & medical AI

Exploring clinical prediction models and medical AI for prostate cancer diagnosis, with attention to the data, assumptions and settings that shape their interpretation.

  • Prediction models
  • Medical AI
03

Evaluation for clinical use

Focusing on external validation, calibration and clinical utility to assess how predictions perform beyond their development data and whether they can support clinical decisions.

  • External validation
  • Calibration
  • Clinical utility
Research perspective

I am interested in predictions that are carefully evaluated, well calibrated and useful for the clinical decisions they aim to support.
